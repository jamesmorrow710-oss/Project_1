Japan has a range of activities for visitors to enjoy. Whether you're interested in exploring ancient temples, experiencing in my opinion one of the best cuisines, or enjoying modern entertainment, such as Disney Tokyo or Universal Studios Japan, there's something for everyone in this country.

Activities I Enjoyed

During my trips to Japan, I enjoyed several unique activities. One of the my favorites was visiting a tea house in a bamboo forest, where I enjoyed a fresh bowl of matcha tea in an open air setting.

I also enjoyed visiting a pig cafe in Osaka with my mother. We sat and cuddled with cute pigs, it was fun to watch them play and interact with each other.
