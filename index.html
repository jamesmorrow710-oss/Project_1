Welcome to my travel blog! Here, I will share my experiences and adventures from my trips to Japan. From the bustling streets of Tokyo to the serene temples of Kyoto, join me as I explore the rich culture, delicious food, and breathtaking landscapes of this beautiful country.

Context

In Highschool I started learning Japanese and became interested in the culture. So as a graduation gift my mother took me on a trip to Japan in the spring of 2023. And the trip was just as amazing as I had hoped! So much so that I decided to go again and take a friend with me in the summer of 2025. And that trip was even better than the first! I made amazing memories and had unforgettable experiences. And I can't wait to go back again!
